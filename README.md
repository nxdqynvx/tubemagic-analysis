## How to Use TubeMagic: Step-by-Step

Here's how a typical session runs from zero to a finished script.

1. **Create your account.** Go to TubeMagic and sign up. The checkout uses Digistore24 for secure payment processing.
2. **Connect your channel.** Paste your YouTube channel link into the dashboard. TubeMagic scans your existing videos to build a voice profile.
3. **Pick your starting point.** Either enter a video topic directly, or use the Video Idea Generator to find something with search demand in your niche.
4. **Open the AI Script Writer.** Select a template type: General Script, How-To, Shorts, or Outline.
5. **Choose your AI model.** Claude or GPT-4o. If you want more natural-sounding narration, Claude is the recommended pick.
6. **Set your word count.** Use the slider to match your target video length. TubeMagic maps the word count to minutes automatically.
7. **Add inspiration videos (optional).** Paste links to 1–3 top-performing videos you want to draw structure from.
8. **Generate.** The full script — hook, body, outro — appears in seconds.
9. **Optimize your metadata.** Run the Warp Upload tool with your unlisted video link to auto-generate a title, description, and tags.

The entire sequence usually takes under an hour once you settle into the rhythm.  For experienced users, it's closer to 20–30 minutes per video.
